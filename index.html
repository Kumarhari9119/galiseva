How GaliSeva Works

  1. Service providers turn ON their availability
  2. Their live location is shared in nearby areas
  3. Consumers receive alerts & view details
  4. Direct call or booking – no middleman

Why GaliSeva?

Hyperlocal & Real-Time

Unlike big platforms, GaliSeva focuses on nearby roaming service providers who are active in your area right now.

No Middlemen

Direct connection between consumer and service provider. No commissions, no delays, no hidden charges.

Designed for Small Towns

Built for Indian streets, mohallas, and towns where services move, not stay fixed on a map.
